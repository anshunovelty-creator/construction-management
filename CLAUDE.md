## Project status (where we left off: 2026-11-03)

- **What this is:** a construction expense ledger for one owner running two firms (AB Infra, Jayraj Infra) across client sites, so that no rupee goes unaccounted for. Full requirements and every decision made so far are in the approved design spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-construction-ledger-design.md`.
- **Stack (approved):** Next.js + TypeScript installable web app (PWA, Android), PostgreSQL on Supabase, Vercel. Bill photos are deferred until the owner raises them again.
- **Build plan:** Phase 1 is split into 5 plans. **Plan 1 (ledger core) is done** (2026-10-23) on branch `feat/ledger-core`: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-22-phase1-plan1-ledger-core.md`. 61 tests pass on PGlite (Node 22 works; no Docker needed).
- **Plan 2 (app shell, users, masters, opening import) is done** and live on Vercel (https://infra-ledger.vercel.app; deploys from `main`), with money entry screens, party/client pages and a materials list built on top. Login is email + password (owner decision 2026-10-23).
- **Firm switcher (done):** a top-right button sets each person's working firm (stored in `working_firms`, switched via `app_set_my_firm`); every screen and new entry follows it. Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-firm-switcher-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-firm-switcher.md`. The three-way (AB / Jayraj / Combined) reports come with the reports work.
- **Quick entry (done 2026-10-26):** a vendor's page has "+ Add bill" / "+ Pay" cards that save in place; payments show what is still owed with "Pay full"; every rupee box says the amount back in words (`src/lib/words.ts`).
- **English only (owner decision 2026-10-28):** Hindi and the language setting were removed; the `profiles.language` column is left in the database, unused (defaults to `en`).
- **Bills are money only (owner decision 2026-11-03; replaces the item-wise bills of 2026-10-29):** the owner types a vendor or client bill as one amount before GST plus one GST % (Setup → GST rates), no item lines. GST is kept apart from cost and income: `gst_input` (vendor bills, claimed back) and `gst_output` (client bills, owed); TDS/retention/deposit are worked out on the bill before GST. Old itemised bills keep their rows in the append-only `bill_items` table (shown on the entry page) but no longer count as stock. TDS on bills (owner decision 2026-10-29): a TDS rate from Setup → TDS rates is worked out on the amount before GST. A vendor bill’s TDS is owed to the government (tds_payable) and the vendor is paid less; a client’s TDS is claimable (tds_claimable). Each bill keeps its rate and section in the append-only bill_tds table, shown on the TDS record page (/tds).
- **Work orders set aside (owner decision 2026-10-29):** a client bill names the client and the site; a receipt names the client. The `work_orders` table stays for old entries only.
- **Stock and daily report (2026-10-30, changed 2026-11-03):** supervisors keep the stock. They type what arrives at their site (`stock_in`: material, quantity, optional rate) on Stock → Arrived; a material's kind in Setup decides whether it is stock (`stock_in_rows`) or a company asset (`asset_in_rows`, Assets page, with moves between sites). Supervisors report material used per site per day on `/report` (`material_use` table); it comes out of that site's stock, valued at the average rate of arrivals that had a rate (`src/lib/stock.ts`). Supervisors also keep Setup → Workers, Materials, Units and Combination works. The Stock page is a register per site and period (opening, arrived, used, left, value) with Arrived and Used tabs; each material opens a passbook of its day-by-day movement (`/stock/material`). A material missing from the list can be added from the arrival form or the daily report. Over-use is allowed and shown in red. Each used line may carry its own rate (`material_use.rate_paise`, filled with the site's average and changeable); left empty, it is valued at the average. Units are a Setup list (`units`); each material picks one, and each unit says how it is measured (`measure`: quantity / length / area / volume). Work done (`work_done`) is typed per activity (description, unit, rate) with measurement lines of No. × length × width × height as the unit needs (`src/lib/work.ts`); the server works out quantity and amount again. Repeated measurements of one activity are numbered 1.1, 1.2… (`lineSerial`). Supervisors see rates and amounts; material rates reach them through `app_site_bought`. The daily report also has labour and staff (attendance, OT), other costs and the day's profit-loss, printable like the site sheet (sample: `data/private/SVG SITE WORK REPORT (18-10-2026)1.pdf`).
- **Combination works (2026-11-03):** PCC, RCC, plaster, tiles… are kept in Setup → Combination works (`mixes`, `mix_items`: how much of each material 1 unit of the work takes), by the owner, office or supervisors. On the daily report, picking one adds it as a group: the work's name as a heading with a line per material inside (ratio shown as a guide), more materials can be added to the group, and other materials sit below under "Other materials". The name is kept on each line (`material_use.mix_name`). The group has a "Work done today" box (owner decision 2026-11-17, replacing typing each quantity by hand): typing e.g. 1.5 fills each of the work's materials with 1.5 × what 1 unit takes; the filled quantities can still be changed, lines added by hand are left alone, and the work done itself is not saved. They come out of stock like any used line. Only the owner can delete a work (on its change page); saved reports keep its name.
- **Supervisors add no money entries (2026-11-03):** "Add an entry" is gone for them; what they spend goes on the daily report.
- **Ledger (2026-11-03):** a party's or client's ledger is a monthly Tally-style statement: Opening Balance first, bills with their bill number, Closing Balance last. A party's first entry is its opening balance (no separate opening form). Party bills no longer ask the type of cost. The login page has a show-password button.
- **Money words (owner decision 2026-11-05):** red is money we pay, green is money we get. Extra paid to a party or labour worker shows a minus before the rupee sign, in green with the words Extra paid (owner decision 2026-11-06, replacing no minus signs); extra received from a client has none. A balance under ₹1 either way counts as nothing left (`roundOffSmall`, owner decision 2026-11-06): saving any vendor or client entry that leaves or finds such a remainder also posts a Round off entry to clear it for good (`src/lib/ledger/round-off.ts`, against `cash_short_excess`; a payment or receipt line with no money moving). Remainders already there clear on that party's next entry and show as nothing left until then. Labour simply drops it. Vendors show Total to pay, Remaining to pay and Extra paid; clients show Remaining to receive and Extra received. Lists and totals keep to-pay and extra apart (`sumFigures`: `toPay`, `extra`), and an overpaid party has the status `extra`, not `cleared`. Ledgers keep Dr/Cr with the words beneath and state the closing balance in a coloured line above the table.
- **Labour supply section (owner decision 2026-11-06, replaces the attendance cards, staff, PF and ESIC of 2026-11-05):** the client's labour supply business, kept apart from the firms. Setup is a name and a salary per day. `/labour/month` is one sheet per month, one row per worker: Present days (can pass the days in the month; double duty counts as two), OT hours, Advance, Deduction, Addition, Reason, Paid, all in `labour_months`. Salary, To pay, Remaining to pay and Extra paid work themselves out; OT hour = day salary ÷ 8. `/labour/[id]` is the worker's ledger, and what one month leaves carries into the next, so extra paid comes off the next salary (`src/lib/labour.ts`). Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-labour-supply-design.md`. `labour_attendance` and `labour_settings` stay in the database, unused. **Families** (owner decision 2026-11-21): when a whole family works and only the head is paid, Labour supply → Families lists heads; a family's page (`/labour/family/[id]`) takes each member's days at their own salary and keeps money given to the head as dated lines (Paid / Advance / Deduction, in `labour_money`); `familyLedger` in `src/lib/labour.ts` adds it up (`labour_workers.head_id`, migration `20261121100000_labour_families.sql`). Family people stay off the Workers list and monthly sheet. A worker's Change details page has a Family box (not in a family / head of their own / member of a head's family); moving someone takes their months with them, and a head with members or money given stays the head (database trigger). Extra paid to a head stays on record and comes off the next month. The Per day salary is typeable on the monthly and family sheets (owner decision 2026-11-21): a change counts from that month on until changed again, and earlier months keep theirs (`ratesToSave` keeps the earliest salary from the month before when it is the one changed, as earlier months borrow it). A new `labour` login sees only this section (`requireProfile` keeps it out of every page that does not name it; `tests/db/labour-supply.test.ts` proves it reads nothing of the firms); tried in a browser on the practice database 2026-11-06. To test without touching live: `npx supabase migration up --local`, and run the app with `.env.docker.local` values.
- **AJAX machines (owner decision 2026-11-06):** the client's machines out on rent with other parties. `/machines` lists each with who has it, last service and next service; `/machines/[id]` holds the service record: date, running hours, cost, optional note (`machines`, `machine_services`; rules in `src/lib/machines.ts`). Next service = last reading + the machine's hours between services (500 unless changed). A record only: the cost does not post to the ledger or either firm, rent is not tracked, and there is no current meter reading, so nothing warns when a service is overdue. Owner and office only.
- **Money received pop-up (2026-11-06):** a client's page has a "Money received" button (`src/components/receive-money.tsx`): big amount box grouped as typed (`groupRupees`), a Full chip and a Last bill chip (only when below what is left), a bar showing what this receipt does to what is left (red and worded Extra received when it goes past the total), bank/cash cards, Today/Yesterday, then a confirmation with Add another. It saves through `postClientRows`, so posting is unchanged. The Excel-style sheet stays under "Add several at once". The vendor Pay form is not changed.
- **Home, supervisor's day and Pay pop-up (2026-11-17):** signing in lands on Home (`/overview`), first in the sidebar and the phone's tab bar (Day book moved under More); it shows each site's billed, costs and profit before the balances. The sidebar is grouped (Money, Sites, Other business, Admin); Add an entry and its Sheet are out of the menu and off Home for now (owner decision 2026-11-17), though the pages remain at `/entries/new`. A supervisor's Home has a "Fill today's report" button and, per site given to them, which parts of today's report are filled (work done, material used, attendance, other costs). A party's Pay button opens the same pop-up as Money received (`ReceiveMoney` with `side="party"`): grouped amount, Pay full and Last bill chips, the bar, Extra paid in green when it goes past what is owed, the last-used account picked; it saves through `postPartyRows`. The Day book lists entries by their own date under a heading per day, hides cancelled mistakes behind a chip, and no longer repeats the firm's balances.
- **Delete (owner decision 2026-11-17, replaces the testing-only `ALLOW_DELETE` switch):** the owner can delete a wrong or demo Setup record from its change page (`DeleteBox`, `src/components/delete-box.tsx`); sites, parties, workers and bank accounts need their name typed first (`TYPE_TO_DELETE`, `src/lib/delete.ts`), and the server checks the name again. Anything still in use is refused by the database and the page says what uses it. **Firms are never deleted** (owner decision): they can only be renamed. A labour-supply worker can be deleted (name typed) only with no months saved; otherwise untick Active. The owner can delete an AJAX machine with its service record (name typed). Every delete goes to the **recycle bin** (owner decision 2026-11-17; `recycle_bin` table, `app_bin_delete` / `app_bin_restore` / `app_bin_purge`, migration `20261117100000_recycle_bin.sql`, server helper `src/lib/bin.ts`): the record and its own lines (a worker's rates, a machine's services, a work's materials) are kept as JSON for 30 days and put back with the same id from Admin → Recycle bin (`/bin`, owner only). Deleting runs as the signed-in user inside the database function, so the change log names who deleted it. Admin → Recycle bin → Clear demo data (`/bin/demo`) lists every record with the word test or demo in its name and sends the ticked ones to the bin after CONFIRM is typed; what real entries still use is kept and listed. Ledger entries are still never deleted: they are cancelled.
- **Salary: Staff and Labour tabs, PF and a ledger per person (owner decision 2026-11-21):** `/salary` is Staff, `/salary/labour` is Labour (`src/app/(app)/salary/sheet.tsx`). Each row: salary, PF, advance, deduction, net, from before, paid, remaining to pay. PF is a % of salary kept in Setup → PF rates (`pf_rates`, migration `20261121130000_pf_rates.sql`; each rate counts from the month of its date on, earlier months keep theirs; 12% if none; no wage ceiling; owner and office change it, nobody deletes it); staff are cut and labour not by default, and a switch per person (or the column-head switch for the whole tab) counts from the month shown on, until switched again (`salary_pf`). A person's page (`/salary/[id]`) is the slip plus dated money lines (Advance / Deduction with a reason / Salary paid, in `salary_money`) and a month-by-month ledger that carries what each month leaves (`salaryLedger`, `salaryRow` in `src/lib/wages.ts`). Staff earn from the day they were added or first marked; changing a staff salary also changes past months (not kept per month). Salary paid is a record only, not posted to the firm's cash or bank. Migration `20261121120000_salary_ledger.sql`; owner and office only.
